# Results on dark energy

Scope: results that hold independently of one dark-energy model: theorems and no-go results, generic predictions, bounds on model classes, and the history of key results.

Snapshot: 2026-11-03

## The cosmological-constant problem
<!-- key: the-cosmological-constant-problem -->

- Size of the mismatch: a Planck-scale cut-off gives $\rho_{\rm vac}\approx 10^{74}$ GeV⁴ against ρ_Λ ≈ 10⁻⁴⁷ GeV⁴ (Copeland et al., arXiv:hep-th/0603057) [src: Eqs. 67, 70]. A properly renormalised free-field estimate is "far from being 122 orders of magnitude larger than the critical energy density" (Martin, arXiv:1205.3365) [src: abstract]; Martin finds "something like 54 orders of magnitude" [src: Sect. X.2]. Kaya & Lahey (2026) agree that the commonly cited 120 orders of magnitude rest on an estimate "that does not account for regularization and renormalization", while "fundamental principles of quantum field theory nevertheless lead to a huge mismatch", with further conceptual problems in an expanding universe (non-uniqueness of the vacuum, comoving versus physical scales) (arXiv:2606.20778) [src: abstract].
- Dynamical dark energy does not solve the problem: it presumes the vacuum energy is zero "due to some as yet unknown mechanism" (arXiv:hep-th/0603057) [src: Sect. IV.2]; the bound on the vacuum energy from the critical density remains (arXiv:1205.3365) [src: Sect. X.2].
- Anthropic selection: Weinberg "was the first to point out that once the cosmological constant comes to dominate the dynamics of the universe, then structure formation stops", so "structure formation should be completed before the domination of vacuum energy, otherwise there could be no observers now", which leads to a bound "arising out of an anthropic argument" (as summarised in arXiv:hep-th/0603057) [sec: Sect. IV.5].
- Swampland: Obied et al. propose the criterion |∇V| ≥ c·V for a positive constant c in any consistent theory of quantum gravity, which forbids de Sitter vacua (arXiv:1806.08362) [src: abstract]; the status of de Sitter and quintessence constructions in string theory is reviewed by Andriot (arXiv:2603.25797) [src: abstract] and belongs to `thcosmo-fundamental-theory`.

## Quintessence: generic results
<!-- key: quintessence-generic-results -->

- A canonical scalar field has $-1\leq w_{\phi}\leq 1$ (arXiv:hep-th/0603057) [src: Sect. V.1, text after Eq. 106].
- Exponential potential: the field-dominated attractor accelerates for $\lambda^{2}<2$; the scaling attractor exists for $\lambda^{2}>3\gamma$ and does not accelerate (arXiv:hep-th/0603057) [src: Table I; Sect. VI.2.1].
- "Limits of quintessence": "if the scalar field is prohibited from attaining values exceeding the Planck scale", that is $E\equiv\lvert V/(dV/d\phi)\rvert<M_{P}$, there is a lower bound on 1 + w; with Ω_de ≈ 0.7, thawing models must satisfy 1 + w ≳ 0.004 and freezing models 1 + w ≳ 0.01, and "this may be the limit of quintessence", about a 0.2% difference from Λ in distance to z = 1 (Caldwell & Linder, arXiv:astro-ph/0505494) [src: abstract; inline, text after Fig. 2]; telling thawing from freezing needs "a measurement resolution of order σ(w') ≈ (1+w)" [src: text after Fig. 1]. Parameters "may be finely tuned to keep 1+w arbitrarily close to zero" within the regions [src: text after Fig. 1].

## The phantom divide
<!-- key: the-phantom-divide -->

- No-go theorem: for a dark energy with "an arbitrary scalar-field Lagrangian" that "dominates in the flat Friedmann universe", Vikman finds that "generally such transitions are physically implausible", and without domination the result "still holds for a certain class of dark energy Lagrangians, in particular, for Lagrangians quadratic in field's first derivatives" (Vikman, arXiv:astro-ph/0407107) [src: abstract]. Ren et al. state it for "basic single perfect fluid models or single-field models" $L(\phi,\partial_\mu\phi\partial^\mu\phi)$ "without higher derivatives" minimally coupled in GR, for which "w cannot cross the cosmological constant boundary -1" [src: text before Eq. 2], with the fluid perturbation argument (arXiv:2606.01360) [src: text after Eq. 10].
- Ghosts: "assuming only that ghosts interact at least gravitationally", a Lorentz-violating cutoff Λ < 3 MeV "for consistency with the cosmic gamma ray background"; "theories of ghosts with a Lorentz-conserving cutoff are completely excluded" (Cline, Jeon & Moore, arXiv:hep-ph/0311312) [src: abstract].
- Related no-go and obstruction results in 2025–2026: in scale-invariant scalar-tensor theories with non-minimal derivative coupling, phantom crossing violates "the no-ghost stability requirement", and "the swampland de Sitter and distance conjectures independently obstruct phantom crossing" (Baghbani, arXiv:2610.14354) [src: abstract]; Brax finds phantom crossing "a natural feature" of dilaton dark energy, with the deviation from −1 "limited by screening locally", an obstruction that "extends to all single-field screened models of the chameleon-type" with "a negligible variation of the equation of state at low redshift" (Brax, arXiv:2507.16723) [src: abstract]. Crossing realised "while avoiding ghost and gradient instabilities in linear perturbations": a spatially covariant model ("Freezing Gravity") whose scalar sound speed tends to infinity on large scales and which has an energy cutoff (Yao, Ye & Silvestri, arXiv:2508.01378) [src: abstract]; kinetic gravity braiding (arXiv:2512.13691) and Horndeski with broken shift symmetry (arXiv:2508.17231) belong to `thcosmo-modified-gravity`.

## After DESI: what the data ask of a theory
<!-- key: after-desi-what-the-data-ask-of-a-theory -->

The measured $w_0$, $w_a$ belong to cosmoskill `cosmo-dark-energy-modified-gravity`; this section records only what the theory literature takes from them.

- Shape asked for: posteriors in the quadrant $w_{0}>-1,w_{a}<0$, "a quintom-B type evolution" from phantom-like in the past to quintessence-like today (Li et al. review, arXiv:2606.21826) [src: Sect. II.2]. Six two-parameter forms (CPL, JBP, BA, EXP, LOG, SIN) keep the preference, with "the precise statistical significance" sensitive to the SN compilation, DES Y5 strengthening and Pantheon+ weakening it [src: Sect. II.2]; a DESI DR2 extended analysis with other parameterisations and non-parametric methods finds "similar dynamical trends and phantom crossing behaviors" [src: Sect. II.2].
- Size of the preference, as one critical review states it: "mild-to-moderate", "below the 3σ level in most cases", up to ∼3.8σ with DESI DR2 + CMB + DES Y5 or Union3; the DES Y5 driven preference is attributed to low-z SNe Ia "affected by calibration systematics", the Bayesian evidence dropping to inconclusive ($\vert\Delta\ln\mathcal{Z}\vert<1$) without them (Capozziello et al., arXiv:2512.10585) [src: Sect. V]. Turyshev states the preference is "particularly sensitive to redshift-dependent SN calibration/selection residuals at the few×10⁻² mag level" (arXiv:2602.05368) [src: abstract].
- Dynamical dark energy and H0: dark energy is negligible at recombination, so it "cannot modify early-time physics or reduce the sound horizon"; w(z) > −1 today "drives H0 towards lower values", a behaviour the authors call "generic" (arXiv:2512.10585) [src: Sect. V].
- What can produce the shape in GR: a canonical field "ranges in the region" −1 ≤ w ≤ 1 (arXiv:hep-th/0603057, Sect. V.1), and Vikman finds single-field crossings "generally" "physically implausible" (arXiv:astro-ph/0407107, abstract). Routes in the literature: (i) thawing quintessence read through a different parameterisation, or with curvature, where "the data do not enforce phantom crossing" (arXiv:2504.15190) [src: abstract]; (ii) multi-field and string-motivated quintessence that "can appear phantom" in a CPL fit (arXiv:2511.23463) [src: abstract]; (iii) a dark-sector interaction that mimics a crossing in $w_{\rm de}^{\rm eff}$ (arXiv:2606.21826, Eq. 9); (iv) a genuine two-field quintom, carrying a ghost (arXiv:2606.01360, Eq. 11); (v) negative dark-energy densities, where the null-energy-condition boundary replaces w = −1 (arXiv:2602.21169) [src: abstract]; (vi) modified gravity: `thcosmo-modified-gravity`. "Within the GR-based effective-fluid mapping", model-agnostic reconstructions of ρ_DE find a sign change from negative to positive density, which "a single canonical scalar cannot realize", with w₀ ≃ −1 when SNe are included (Akarsu et al., arXiv:2602.08928) [src: abstract].
- A parameter-free example: Bound Dark Energy, the lightest meson of a dark SU(3) with an inverse power-law potential, keeps w > −1 throughout and predicts $w_0 = -0.9298 \pm 0.0003$ (de la Macorra & Lozano Torres, arXiv:2601.08943) [src: abstract].
