# Results on dark energy

Scope: results that hold independently of one dark-energy model: theorems and no-go results, generic predictions, bounds on model classes, and the history of key results.

Snapshot: 2026-11-03

## The cosmological-constant problem
<!-- key: the-cosmological-constant-problem -->

- Size of the mismatch: a Planck-scale cut-off gives $\rho_{\rm vac}\approx 10^{74}$ GeV⁴ against ρ_Λ ≈ 10⁻⁴⁷ GeV⁴ (Copeland et al., arXiv:hep-th/0603057) [src: Eqs. 67, 70]. A properly renormalised free-field estimate is "far from being 122 orders of magnitude larger than the critical energy density" (Martin, arXiv:1205.3365) [src: abstract]; Martin finds "something like 54 orders of magnitude" [src: Sect. X.2]. Kaya & Lahey (2026) agree that the commonly cited 120 orders of magnitude rest on an estimate "that does not account for regularization and renormalization", while "fundamental principles of quantum field theory nevertheless lead to a huge mismatch", with further conceptual problems in an expanding universe (non-uniqueness of the vacuum, comoving versus physical scales) (arXiv:2606.20778) [src: abstract].
- Dynamical dark energy does not solve the problem: it presumes the vacuum energy is zero "due to some as yet unknown mechanism" (arXiv:hep-th/0603057) [src: Sect. IV.2]; the bound on the vacuum energy from the critical density remains (arXiv:1205.3365) [src: Sect. X.2].
- Anthropic selection: Weinberg was the first to point out that structure formation must be completed before vacuum energy dominates, which bounds Λ from above in an anthropic argument (as summarised in arXiv:hep-th/0603057) [sec: Sect. IV.5].
- Swampland: Obied et al. propose the criterion |∇V| ≥ c·V for a positive constant c in any consistent theory of quantum gravity, which forbids de Sitter vacua (arXiv:1806.08362) [src: abstract]; the status of de Sitter and quintessence constructions in string theory is reviewed by Andriot (arXiv:2603.25797) [src: abstract] and belongs to `thcosmo-fundamental-theory`.

## Quintessence: generic results
<!-- key: quintessence-generic-results -->

- A canonical scalar field has $-1\leq w_{\phi}\leq 1$ (arXiv:hep-th/0603057) [src: Sect. V.1, text after Eq. 106]; phantom behaviour needs a non-canonical or coupled field, or a modification of gravity.
- Exponential potential: the field-dominated attractor accelerates for $\lambda^{2}<2$; the scaling attractor exists for $\lambda^{2}>3\gamma$ and does not accelerate (arXiv:hep-th/0603057) [src: Table I; Sect. VI.2.1].
- "Limits of quintessence": with Ω_de ≈ 0.7, thawing models must satisfy 1 + w ≳ 0.004 and freezing models 1 + w ≳ 0.01, about a 0.2% difference from Λ in distance to z = 1 (Caldwell & Linder, arXiv:astro-ph/0505494) [src: text after Fig. 2]; telling thawing from freezing needs "a measurement resolution of order σ(w') ≈ (1+w)" [src: text after Fig. 1]. Parameters "may be finely tuned to keep 1+w arbitrarily close to zero" within the regions [src: text after Fig. 1].

## The phantom divide
<!-- key: the-phantom-divide -->

- No-go theorem: single perfect fluid, or single field $L(\phi,\partial_\mu\phi\partial^\mu\phi)$ minimally coupled in GR, cannot cross w = −1 stably (Vikman, arXiv:astro-ph/0407107) [src: abstract]; restated with the fluid perturbation argument by Ren et al. (arXiv:2606.01360) [src: text after Eq. 10].
- Ghosts with a Lorentz-conserving cutoff are "completely excluded"; with a Lorentz-violating cutoff Λ < 3 MeV (Cline, Jeon & Moore, arXiv:hep-ph/0311312) [src: abstract].
- Generalisations of the no-go in 2025–2026: scale-invariant scalar-tensor theories with non-minimal derivative coupling cannot cross without a ghost, and the swampland conjectures independently obstruct the crossing (Baghbani, arXiv:2610.14354) [src: abstract]; for screened single-field models of the chameleon type the variation of w at low redshift is negligible (Brax, arXiv:2507.16723) [src: abstract]. Crossing realised without ghost or gradient instability: a spatially covariant model ("Freezing Gravity") whose scalar sound speed tends to infinity on large scales and which has an energy cutoff (Yao, Ye & Silvestri, arXiv:2508.01378) [src: abstract]; kinetic gravity braiding (arXiv:2512.13691) and Horndeski with broken shift symmetry (arXiv:2508.17231) belong to `thcosmo-modified-gravity`.

## After DESI: what the data ask of a theory
<!-- key: after-desi-what-the-data-ask-of-a-theory -->

The measured $w_0$, $w_a$ belong to cosmoskill `cosmo-dark-energy-modified-gravity`; this section records only what the theory literature takes from them.

- Shape asked for: posteriors in the quadrant $w_{0}>-1,w_{a}<0$, "a quintom-B type evolution" from phantom-like in the past to quintessence-like today (Li et al. review, arXiv:2606.21826) [src: Sect. II.2]. Six two-parameter forms (CPL, JBP, BA, EXP, LOG, SIN) keep the preference, with "the precise statistical significance" sensitive to the SN compilation, DES Y5 strengthening and Pantheon+ weakening it [src: Sect. II.2]; a DESI DR2 extended analysis with other parameterisations and non-parametric methods finds "similar dynamical trends and phantom crossing behaviors" [src: Sect. II.2].
- Size of the preference, as one critical review states it: "mild-to-moderate", "below the 3σ level in most cases", up to ∼3.8σ with DESI DR2 + CMB + DES Y5 or Union3; the DES Y5 driven preference is attributed to low-z SNe Ia "affected by calibration systematics", the Bayesian evidence dropping to inconclusive ($\vert\Delta\ln\mathcal{Z}\vert<1$) without them (Capozziello et al., arXiv:2512.10585) [src: Sect. V]. Turyshev states the preference is "particularly sensitive to redshift-dependent SN calibration/selection residuals at the few×10⁻² mag level" (arXiv:2602.05368) [src: abstract].
- Dynamical dark energy and H0: dark energy is negligible at recombination, so it "cannot modify early-time physics or reduce the sound horizon"; w(z) > −1 today "drives H0 towards lower values", a behaviour the authors call "generic" (arXiv:2512.10585) [src: Sect. V].
- What can produce the shape in GR: a single canonical field cannot cross w = −1 (arXiv:hep-th/0603057, Sect. V.1; arXiv:astro-ph/0407107, abstract). Routes in the literature: (i) thawing quintessence read through a different parameterisation, or with curvature, where "the data do not enforce phantom crossing" (arXiv:2504.15190) [src: abstract]; (ii) multi-field and string-motivated quintessence that "can appear phantom" in a CPL fit (arXiv:2511.23463) [src: abstract]; (iii) a dark-sector interaction that mimics a crossing in $w_{\rm de}^{\rm eff}$ (arXiv:2606.21826, Eq. 9); (iv) a genuine two-field quintom, carrying a ghost (arXiv:2606.01360, Eq. 11); (v) negative dark-energy densities, where the null-energy-condition boundary replaces w = −1 (arXiv:2602.21169) [src: abstract]; (vi) modified gravity: `thcosmo-modified-gravity`. Model-agnostic reconstructions of ρ_DE in GR find a sign change from negative to positive density, which "a single canonical scalar cannot realize", with w₀ ≃ −1 when SNe are included (Akarsu et al., arXiv:2602.08928) [src: abstract].
- A parameter-free example: Bound Dark Energy, the lightest meson of a dark SU(3) with an inverse power-law potential, keeps w > −1 throughout and predicts $w_0 = -0.9298 \pm 0.0003$ (de la Macorra & Lozano Torres, arXiv:2601.08943) [src: abstract].
