# Results on dark energy

Scope: results that hold independently of one dark-energy model: theorems and no-go results, generic predictions, bounds on model classes, and the history of key results.

Snapshot: 2026-11-03

## The cosmological-constant problem
<!-- key: the-cosmological-constant-problem -->

- Size of the mismatch: a Planck-scale cut-off gives $\rho_{\rm vac}\approx 10^{74}$ GeV⁴ against ρ_Λ ≈ 10⁻⁴⁷ GeV⁴ (Copeland et al., arXiv:hep-th/0603057) [src: Eqs. 67, 70]. A properly renormalised free-field estimate is "far from being 122 orders of magnitude larger than the critical energy density" (Martin, arXiv:1205.3365) [src: abstract]; Martin finds "something like 54 orders of magnitude" [src: Sect. X.2]. Kaya & Lahey (2026) point out that the "commonly cited discrepancy of 120 orders of magnitude" "is based on an estimate that does not account for regularization and renormalization", while "fundamental principles of quantum field theory nevertheless lead to a huge mismatch", and add "conceptual challenges related to cosmic expansion, such as the choice between comoving and physical scales in certain contexts and the non-uniqueness of vacuum" (arXiv:2606.20778) [src: abstract].
- Dynamical dark energy does not solve the problem: it presumes the vacuum energy is zero "due to some as yet unknown mechanism" (arXiv:hep-th/0603057) [src: Sect. IV.2]; "even if the source of the acceleration is another fluid, we still have the constraint" ρ_vac < ρ_cri (arXiv:1205.3365) [src: Sect. X.2].
- Anthropic selection: Weinberg "was the first to point out that once the cosmological constant comes to dominate the dynamics of the universe, then structure formation stops", so "structure formation should be completed before the domination of vacuum energy, otherwise there could be no observers now", which leads to a bound "arising out of an anthropic argument" (as summarised in arXiv:hep-th/0603057) [sec: Sect. IV.5].
- Swampland: Obied et al. propose the criterion |∇V| ≥ c·V for a positive constant c in any consistent theory of quantum gravity, which forbids de Sitter vacua (arXiv:1806.08362) [src: abstract]; the status of de Sitter and quintessence constructions in string theory is reviewed by Andriot (arXiv:2603.25797) [src: abstract] and belongs to `thcosmo-fundamental-theory`.

## Quintessence: generic results
<!-- key: quintessence-generic-results -->

- A canonical scalar field has $-1\leq w_{\phi}\leq 1$ (arXiv:hep-th/0603057) [src: Sect. V.1, text after Eq. 106].
- Exponential potential: the field-dominated attractor accelerates for $\lambda^{2}<2$; the scaling attractor exists for $\lambda^{2}>3\gamma$ and does not accelerate (arXiv:hep-th/0603057) [src: Table I; Sect. VI.2.1].
- "Limits of quintessence": "if the scalar field is prohibited from attaining values exceeding the Planck scale", that is $E\equiv\lvert V/(dV/d\phi)\rvert<M_{P}$, there is a lower bound on 1 + w; with Ω_de ≈ 0.7, thawing models must satisfy 1 + w ≳ 0.004 and freezing models 1 + w ≳ 0.01, and "this may be the limit of quintessence", about a 0.2% difference from Λ in distance to z = 1 (Caldwell & Linder, arXiv:astro-ph/0505494) [src: abstract; inline, text after Fig. 2]; the results indicate "a desired measurement resolution of order σ(w') ≈ (1+w)" [src: abstract]. Parameters "may be finely tuned to keep 1+w arbitrarily close to zero" within the regions [src: text after Fig. 1].

## The phantom divide
<!-- key: the-phantom-divide -->

- No-go theorem: for a dark energy with "an arbitrary scalar-field Lagrangian" that "dominates in the flat Friedmann universe", Vikman finds that "generally such transitions are physically implausible", and without domination the result "still holds for a certain class of dark energy Lagrangians, in particular, for Lagrangians quadratic in field's first derivatives" (Vikman, arXiv:astro-ph/0407107) [src: abstract]. Ren et al. state it for "basic single perfect fluid models or single-field models" $L(\phi,\partial_\mu\phi\partial^\mu\phi)$ "without higher derivatives" minimally coupled in GR, for which "w cannot cross the cosmological constant boundary -1" [src: text before Eq. 2], with the fluid perturbation argument (arXiv:2606.01360) [src: text after Eq. 10].
- Ghosts: "assuming only that ghosts interact at least gravitationally", a Lorentz-violating cutoff Λ < 3 MeV "for consistency with the cosmic gamma ray background"; "theories of ghosts with a Lorentz-conserving cutoff are completely excluded" (Cline, Jeon & Moore, arXiv:hep-ph/0311312) [src: abstract].
- Related no-go and obstruction results in 2025–2026: in scale-invariant scalar-tensor theories with non-minimal derivative coupling, phantom crossing violates "the no-ghost stability requirement", and "the swampland de Sitter and distance conjectures independently obstruct phantom crossing" (Baghbani, arXiv:2610.14354) [src: abstract]; Brax finds phantom crossing "a natural feature" of dilaton dark energy, with the deviation from −1 "limited by screening locally", an obstruction that "extends to all single-field screened models of the chameleon-type" with "a negligible variation of the equation of state at low redshift" (Brax, arXiv:2507.16723) [src: abstract]. A spatially covariant model ("Freezing Gravity") realises "arbitrary background evolutions while avoiding ghost and gradient instabilities in linear perturbations"; its scalar sound speed "approaches infinity at large scale", and "the theory has a cut-off in energy, signaled by the pole in the speed of sound, when the effective Planck mass exceeds Planck mass" (Yao, Ye & Silvestri, arXiv:2508.01378) [src: abstract]; kinetic gravity braiding (arXiv:2512.13691) and Horndeski with broken shift symmetry (arXiv:2508.17231) belong to `thcosmo-modified-gravity`.

## After DESI: what the data ask of a theory
<!-- key: after-desi-what-the-data-ask-of-a-theory -->

The measured $w_0$, $w_a$ belong to cosmoskill `cosmo-dark-energy-modified-gravity`; this section records only what the theory literature takes from them.

- Shape asked for: "the preferred parameter regions systematically lie in the quadrant" $w_{0}>-1,w_{a}<0$, "corresponding to a quintom-B type evolution" from phantom-like in the past to quintessence-like today (Li et al. review, arXiv:2606.21826) [src: Sect. II.2]. Across six two-parameter forms (CPL, JBP, BA, EXP, LOG, SIN) the preference "does not disappear when the CPL assumption is relaxed", with "the precise statistical significance" sensitive to the SN compilation: "in general, the DESY5 dataset tends to strengthen the preference for DDE, while PantheonPlus often leads to a comparatively weaker deviation" [src: Sect. II.2]; a DESI DR2 extended analysis with other parameterisations and non-parametric methods finds "similar dynamical trends and phantom crossing behaviors" [src: Sect. II.2].
- Size of the preference, as one critical review states it: "mild-to-moderate", "below the 3σ level in most cases", up to ∼3.8σ with DESI DR2 + CMB + DES Y5 or Union3; the DES Y5 driven preference is attributed to low-z SNe Ia "affected by calibration systematics", the Bayesian evidence dropping to "an inconclusive statistical preference" ($\vert\Delta\ln\mathcal{Z}\vert<1$) when they are excluded (Capozziello et al., arXiv:2512.10585) [src: Sect. V; Sect. IV.5]. Turyshev states the preference is "particularly sensitive to redshift-dependent SN calibration/selection residuals at the few×10⁻² mag level" (arXiv:2602.05368) [src: abstract].
- Dynamical dark energy and H0: "as dark energy is negligible at recombination, dynamical dark energy cannot modify early-time physics or reduce the sound horizon"; the DESI DR2 preference for ω(z) > −1 "drives H0 towards lower values rather than higher ones", and "this behavior is generic" (arXiv:2512.10585) [src: Sect. V].
- What can produce the shape in GR: a canonical field "ranges in the region" −1 ≤ w ≤ 1 (arXiv:hep-th/0603057, Sect. V.1), and, for a dark energy that "dominates in the flat Friedmann universe", Vikman finds that "generally such transitions are physically implausible" (arXiv:astro-ph/0407107, abstract). Routes in the literature: (i) thawing quintessence read through a different parameterisation, or with curvature, where "the data do not enforce phantom crossing" (arXiv:2504.15190) [src: abstract]; (ii) multi-field and string-motivated quintessence that "can appear phantom" in a CPL fit (arXiv:2511.23463) [src: abstract]; (iii) a dark-sector interaction, which "may even mimic a time-varying or phantom-crossing EoS" in $w_{\rm de}^{\rm eff}$ (arXiv:2606.21826, Eq. 9; Sect. III.1); (iv) a genuine two-field quintom, carrying a ghost (arXiv:2606.01360, Eq. 11); (v) negative dark-energy densities, where the null-energy-condition boundary replaces w = −1 (arXiv:2602.21169) [src: abstract]; (vi) modified gravity: `thcosmo-modified-gravity`. "Within the GR-based effective-fluid mapping", "the inferred dark-energy density changes sign for all dataset combinations explored", from negative to positive, and "a single canonical scalar cannot realize such a smooth evolution during expansion"; "combinations including supernovae give" w₀ ≃ −1 (Akarsu et al., arXiv:2602.08928) [src: abstract].
- An example whose "dark energy sector contains no free parameters": Bound Dark Energy, the lightest meson of a supersymmetric SU(3) dark gauge group with an inverse power-law potential, "approaching $w_0 = -0.9298 \pm 0.0003$ at present, with $w > -1$ maintained throughout cosmic history" (de la Macorra & Lozano Torres, arXiv:2601.08943) [src: abstract].
