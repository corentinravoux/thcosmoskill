## Live debates

### How large is the cosmological-constant problem, and is it solved by any mechanism?
<!-- key: how-large-is-the-cosmological-constant-problem -->

- Size: the quoted 120–122 orders of magnitude use a cut-off; renormalised estimates give a smaller but still huge mismatch (arXiv:1205.3365, Sect. X.2; arXiv:2606.20778, abstract).
- Sequestering: Kaloper & Padilla make the dimensional parameters of the matter sector functionals of the 4-volume, which "completely sequesters from gravity all of the vacuum energy from a matter sector, including all loop corrections"; the universe must be finite in spacetime, w_DE ≃ −1 is "a transient", and the universe "will collapse in the future" (arXiv:1309.6562) [src: abstract]. A 2026 variant obtains a sequestering-like global constraint from a lapse function in a 5d anisotropic theory, with radiative contributions "cancelled at all orders" (Khoury, Muntz & Padilla, arXiv:2604.08659) [src: abstract].
- Unimodular gravity de-gravitates the vacuum energy but "traditionally trades the problem of vacuum energy for a fine-tuning of initial conditions" (Kaplan & Rajendran, arXiv:2511.12897) [src: abstract].
- Relaxation: mechanisms that dynamically relax the vacuum energy act on other slow physics; in "yoga" relaxation models conventional QCD axions "are unlikely to remain viable in their standard form" (van de Bruck, Burgess & Smith, arXiv:2603.20008) [src: abstract].

### Do the data prefer thawing quintessence?
<!-- key: do-the-data-prefer-thawing-quintessence -->

- No, once priors are physical: the preference appears only with priors "heavily informed by the data itself" (Payeur, McDonough & Brandenberger, arXiv:2411.13637, v4) [src: abstract].
- Yes, with theory-motivated priors on the Padé-w parameters: "consistently preferred" with DESI DR2 + Planck+ACT + any major SN compilation, including DES-Dovekie, but "contingent on the inclusion of supernovae" (Shlivko, arXiv:2512.20832) [src: abstract].
- `[der]` The two papers differ in the parameterisation of thawing and the data release; the dependence on SN Ia is common ground.

### Is the DESI preference for dynamical dark energy physical, and does it require a phantom phase?
<!-- key: is-the-desi-preference-for-dynamical-dark -->

- For a genuine late-time signal: alternative parameterisations do not remove it (arXiv:2606.21826) [src: Sect. II.2]; an EDE component cannot absorb it once DESI BAO are included, a "robust signature" per Malekjani & Pourojaghi (arXiv:2608.19432) [src: abstract].
- Against, or for caution: driven by low-z DES Y5 SNe Ia (arXiv:2512.10585) [src: Sect. V]; sensitive to SN calibration residuals (arXiv:2602.05368) [src: abstract].
- Phantom phase: "an artifact of a parametrization that is not based on a physical model" per Dinda & Maartens (arXiv:2504.15190) [src: abstract]; KMIX fits without "true phantom behavior" (arXiv:2511.23463) [src: abstract]; interacting dark energy gives only an apparent crossing (arXiv:2606.21826) [src: Sect. III.1]. Against: genuine crossings built without ghosts in modified gravity (`thcosmo-modified-gravity`) or with a spatially covariant scalar (arXiv:2508.01378) [src: abstract].

### Is interacting dark energy preferred, and by which kernel?
<!-- key: is-interacting-dark-energy-preferred-and-by -->

- For: Q = βH₀ρ_de at 3.4σ with SPT + DESI DR2 + DES Y5 (arXiv:2510.11363) [src: abstract].
- Against: 2.1σ with Pantheon+ instead (arXiv:2510.11363) [src: abstract]; sign-switching solutions imply negative past dark-energy densities "which may be unphysical" (arXiv:2601.03122) [src: abstract]; with GRBs and DESI DR2, no significant deviation from ΛCDM (Zhu et al., arXiv:2511.16032) [src: abstract].

## Unresolved calculations
<!-- key: unresolved-calculations -->

- A first-principles interaction kernel Q: "in the absence of a fundamental theory the quantity Q cannot be derived from first principles" (arXiv:1603.08299) [src: Sect. 2.1].
- The vacuum energy beyond free fields and flat space: Martin's estimate is for a free theory (arXiv:1205.3365) [src: abstract]; expanding-universe ambiguities (choice of vacuum, comoving or physical cut-off) remain (arXiv:2606.20778) [src: abstract].
- A UV-complete early-dark-energy potential without lower harmonics (arXiv:2302.09032) [src: Sect. VII.2].
- Scalar-field realisations of sign-switching histories: some have none in a one-field action (arXiv:2609.09261) [src: abstract].

## Stale claims and misconceptions
<!-- key: stale-claims-and-misconceptions -->

- "The vacuum energy is 120 (or 122) orders of magnitude too large": a cut-off estimate; renormalised estimates give a smaller but still huge mismatch (arXiv:1205.3365, Sect. X.2; arXiv:2606.20778, abstract).
- "Dynamical dark energy solves the cosmological-constant problem": it does not (arXiv:hep-th/0603057, Sect. IV.2; arXiv:1205.3365, Sect. X.2).
- "A CPL fit crossing w = −1 shows phantom dark energy": a single canonical field cannot cross, and several non-phantom models fit as well (arXiv:2504.15190; arXiv:2511.23463) [src: abstracts].
- "Dynamical dark energy after DESI eases the H0 tension": w > −1 today lowers H0 (arXiv:2512.10585) [src: Sect. V].
- "Constant-w interacting fluids are fine at the background level, so they are viable": they can be unstable at the perturbation level (arXiv:0804.0232) [src: abstract].
