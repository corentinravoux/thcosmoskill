# Results on modified gravity

Scope: results that hold independently of one model: theorems and no-go results, the tensor-speed constraint and its consequences, stability and positivity bounds, bounds on parameterised frameworks; then the history of the key results.

Snapshot: 2026-11-02

## Theorems and no-go results
<!-- key: theorems-and-no-go-results -->

- **Chameleon no-go (Wang, Hui & Khoury 2012).** Under general conditions, the Compton wavelength of a chameleon-like scalar is at most Mpc at present cosmic density, and the conformal factor relating Einstein- and Jordan-frame scale factors is essentially constant over the last Hubble time; chameleon-like scalars therefore have a negligible effect on linear growth and cannot self-accelerate. The analysis covers chameleon, symmetron and dilaton theories (arXiv:1208.4612) [src: abstract].
- **Degeneracy and Ostrogradski.** Higher time derivatives generically produce Ostrogradski ghosts; degenerate Lagrangians, whose kinetic matrix cannot be inverted, avoid them, and Langlois & Noui classify all degenerate theories quadratic in second derivatives of the scalar (arXiv:1510.06930) [src: abstract].
- **Linear observables and the α basis.** Given $H(t)$ and the matter density today, no linear measurement constrains a Horndeski dark-energy theory beyond the four $\alpha_{i}(t)$, when the scalar rolls monotonically (Bellini & Sawicki, arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Sect. 3.1].

- **Positivity (Adams et al. 2006).** Some local, Lorentz-invariant low-energy EFTs cannot be embedded in a UV theory whose S-matrix obeys canonical analyticity: the signs of a set of leading irrelevant operators must be strictly positive, and the wrong signs give superluminal fluctuations around non-trivial backgrounds. The argument applies to the DGP brane-world model (arXiv:hep-th/0602178, Adams et al. 2006) [src: abstract].
- **Positivity as a cosmological prior.** For Horndeski theories, positivity bounds used as theoretical priors with Planck, SDSS/BOSS and 6dF data "eliminate over 60% of the previously allowed parameter space" in the example class studied, and adding a subluminal speed of gravitational waves "eliminates all but 1%" (Melville & Noller 2019, arXiv:1904.05874) [src: abstract]. Including couplings to standard matter tightens the bounds further, and in the EFT of dark energy model studied a standard UV completion requires gravitational waves to travel superluminally on cosmological backgrounds (de Rham et al. 2021, arXiv:2103.06855) [src: abstract].
- **Positivity in massive gravity.** Applied to $\Lambda_5$ massive gravity, positivity bounds impose the tunings that produce the ghost-free $\Lambda_3$ structure (arXiv:1804.10624) [src: abstract].
- **Stability conditions in the α basis.** No ghost for scalars: D > 0 with $D\equiv\alpha_{\textrm{K}}+\frac{3}{2}\alpha_{\textrm{B}}^{2}$ (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Eq. 14]; no gradient instability: $c_{\rm s}^{2}>0$; tensors: $M_{*}^{2}>0$ (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Eq. 15] and $c_{\text{T}}^{2}=1+\alpha_{\textrm{T}}>0\,.$ (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Sect. 3.3, unnumbered display after Eq. 15]. Gradient instabilities grow on time scales of order the cutoff of the theory [src: Sect. 3.3].

## The speed of tensors after GW170817
<!-- key: the-speed-of-tensors-after-gw170817 -->

- Bound used in the theory literature: $-3\times 10^{-15}<c_{\rm GW}-1<7\times 10^{-16}.$ (arXiv:1901.07183) [src: Eq. 122], from the nearly simultaneous detection of GW170817 and GRB 170817A [src: Sect. 4.2]. Kobayashi notes that gravitational Cherenkov radiation gives a lower bound on $c_{\rm GW}$ that can be tighter, at frequencies much higher than LIGO's [src: Sect. 4.2, footnote 5].
- Consequences in Horndeski theory: $G_{4X}=0$ and $G_{5}=0$ (arXiv:1901.07183) [src: Eq. 124]; in DHOST: $A_{1}=0$, all cubic DHOST excluded [src: Sect. 4.2, text after Eq. 127].
- Graviton decay: the operator $\frac12 \tilde m_4^2(t)\,\delta g^{00}\,({}^{(3)}R+\delta K_\mu^\nu\delta K^\mu_\nu-\delta K^2)$ makes the decay γ → ππ large, so observations force $\tilde m_4=0$, equivalently $\alpha_{\rm H}=0$ (Creminelli et al. 2018, arXiv:1809.03484) [src: abstract]. <!-- eq: notation -->

- Ezquiaga & Zumalacárregui quote |c_g/c − 1| ≤ 5·10⁻¹⁶ from GW170817 and its counterparts, which eliminates late-universe applications of the covariant Galileon and of its Horndeski and most beyond-Horndeski generalisations; three routes remain: the simplest Horndeski terms, a conformal transformation preserving the causal structure, and a background-independent compensation of the terms that modify $c_g$ (arXiv:1710.05901) [src: abstract].
- Creminelli & Vernizzi: to avoid tuning, $c_g$ must stay one on nearby solutions with slightly different matter abundance; of the three functions of quartic and quintic beyond-Horndeski theories only one remains, a conformal coupling to R for Horndeski, and the relations are stable under quantum corrections (arXiv:1710.05877) [src: abstract].
- Baker et al.: stringent constraints on general scalar–tensor and vector–tensor theories, and an independent bound on the graviton mass in bimetric theories (arXiv:1710.06394) [src: abstract].
