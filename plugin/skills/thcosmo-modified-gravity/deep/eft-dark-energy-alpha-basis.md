## Conventions
<!-- key: conventions -->

- Bellini & Sawicki (arXiv:1404.3713) use the (−+++) signature, write ${\cal L}_{3}=-G_{3}(\phi,\,X)\Box\phi$ (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Sect. 2, unnumbered display after Eq. 1] with $X=-\phi^{;\mu}\phi_{;\mu}/2$ (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Sect. 2, inline, text after Eq. 1], cosmic time t with dots, and divide every density by $M_*^2$, marked by a tilde: $\tilde{\rho}_{\text{m}}\equiv\frac{\rho_{\text{m}}}{M_{*}^{2}}\,.$ (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Eq. 8]. Their Planck-mass run rate is $\alpha_{\textrm{M}}\equiv H^{-1}\frac{\mathrm{d}\ln M_{*}^{2}}{\mathrm{d}t}\,.$ (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Eq. 5].
- De Felice & Tsujikawa (arXiv:1110.3878, De Felice & Tsujikawa 2012), the paper Bellini & Sawicki name for the first derivation of the quadratic action (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Sect. 3.3, text before Eq. 13], use the same $-G_3\Box\phi$ and the same X (arXiv:1110.3878) [src: Sect. II, text after Eq. 5], but a scalar metric with a plus sign in the spatial part, $ds^{2}=-(1+2\Psi)dt^{2}+2\partial_{i}\chi\,dt\,dx^{i}+(1+2\Phi)\,d\bm{x}^{2}\,.$ (arXiv:1110.3878) [src: Eq. 14], in the uniform-field gauge $\delta\phi=0$ [src: Sect. III, text after Eq. 14].
- hi_class (arXiv:1605.06102, Zumalacárregui et al. 2016) factors $8\pi G_{\rm N}$ out of the Lagrangians, "reducing the mass dimension of the $G_{i}$ by two", so that "GR is $G_{4}=1/2$ and the rest zero" (arXiv:1605.06102) [src: Sect. 2, footnote 1]; it uses the CLASS normalisation $3\rho_{\text{CLASS}}\equiv 8\pi G_{\text{N}}\rho_{\text{standard}}$, the physical-time Hubble rate H but conformal time τ with primes, so that $a^{\prime}=a^{2}H$ [src: Sect. 2.1, inline, text after Eq. 2.6]. Its $M_*^2$ is dimensionless, "the cosmological strength of gravity" [src: Sect. 2.1, text before Eq. 2.9], and its run rate is $\alpha_{\textrm{M}}\equiv\frac{\mathrm{d}\ln M_{*}^{2}}{\mathrm{d}\ln a}\,,$ (arXiv:1605.06102) [src: Eq. 2.11].
- The Einstein–Boltzmann solver comparison (arXiv:1709.09135, Bellini et al. 2017) writes the Horndeski α functions in cosmic time (its Eq. 8) and the EFT functions in conformal time, with $\alpha_{M}=\left(\ln M_{*}^{2}\right)^{\prime}$ and primes as derivatives with respect to ln a in its App. A (arXiv:1709.09135) [src: App. A, inline, text after Eq. 42].
- Gleyzes, Langlois and Vernizzi (arXiv:1411.3712) define a braiding of their own, which "differs from that introduced in [26] by a factor −2", [26] being Bellini & Sawicki (arXiv:1411.3712) [src: Sect. 3.2.3, footnote 8; References, entry 26]. <!-- num: not a value -->
- No conversion between these conventions is made here beyond the relations the papers state, collected under "Step 6".

## The derivation
<!-- key: the-derivation -->

### Step 1 — the action and the background
<!-- key: step-1-the-action-and-the-background -->

Bellini & Sawicki start from $S=\int\mathrm{d}^{4}x\,\sqrt{-g}\left[\sum_{i=2}^{5}{\cal L}_{i}\,+\mathcal{L}_{\text{m}}[g_{\mu\nu}]\right]\,,$ (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Eq. 1], matter "coupled minimally and universally" [src: Sect. 2]. On the flat FLRW background the role of the Planck mass is played by $M_{*}^{2}(\phi,X,H)\equiv 2\left(G_{4}-2XG_{4X}+XG_{5\phi}-\dot{\phi}HXG_{5X}\right)\,,$ (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Eq. 4], and "If the Planck mass $M_{*}^{2}$ is constant on the solution realised by the Universe then it can have no effect on observables" [src: Sect. 3.2]. The Friedmann equations keep a standard left-hand side, $3H^{2}=\tilde{\rho}_{\text{m}}+\tilde{\mathcal{E}}$ and $2\dot{H}+3H^{2}=-\tilde{p}_{\text{m}}-\tilde{\mathcal{P}}$ (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Eq. 6], with the dark-energy density and pressure of App. A (Eqs. 46–47, not transcribed here).

The pressure contains $\ddot\phi$ through the braiding, $\tilde{\mathcal{P}}\equiv\tilde{P}(\phi,X,H)-\alpha_{\textrm{B}}\frac{H\ddot{\phi}}{\dot{\phi}}\,,$ (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Eq. 9], the "mixing between the scalar and metric kinetic terms" [src: Sect. 3.2, text after Eq. 9]. Because the tilded densities absorb a running $M_*^2$, matter is not conserved in the tilded variables: $\dot{\tilde{\rho}}_{\text{m}}+3H\left(\tilde{\rho}_{\text{m}}+\tilde{p}_{\text{m}}\right)=-\alpha_{\textrm{M}}H\tilde{\rho}_{\text{m}}\,,$ and $\dot{\tilde{\mathcal{E}}}+3H\left(\tilde{\mathcal{E}}+\tilde{\mathcal{P}}\right)=\alpha_{\textrm{M}}H\tilde{\rho}_{\text{m}}\,,$ (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Eq. 10]. The scalar obeys $\dot{n}+3Hn=\mathcal{P}_{\phi}\,,$ (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Eq. 12], with the shift-charge density n of Eq. 49 and the shift-breaking term $\mathcal{P}_\phi$ of Eq. 50. Bellini & Sawicki stress that "despite appearing in background equations, both $\alpha_{\textrm{B}}$ and $\alpha_{\textrm{M}}$ are only possible to determine through their influence on structure formation. Any expansion history can be realised for any choice of these two functions" [src: Sect. 3.2].

The same background in the De Felice–Tsujikawa variables is their Eqs. 7–8 for the lapse and scale-factor variations and $\frac{1}{a^{3}}\frac{d}{dt}\left(a^{3}J\right)=P_{\phi}\,,$ (arXiv:1110.3878) [src: Eq. 9], with the current J of Eq. 10 and $P_\phi$ of Eq. 11; these equations "are not independent because of the Bianchi identities" [src: Sect. II, text after Eq. 13].

### Step 2 — the quadratic action in the uniform-field gauge
<!-- key: step-2-the-quadratic-action-in-the-uniform -->

De Felice & Tsujikawa perturb the action, with two perfect fluids, to second order in the gauge of Eq. 14 and find, after integrations by parts, an action whose coefficients are four functions of the background (arXiv:1110.3878) [src: Eq. 16]:

- $w_{1}\equiv 2\,(G_{{4}}-2\,XG_{{4,X}})-2X\,(G_{{5,X}}{\dot{\phi}}H-G_{{5,\phi}})\,,$ (arXiv:1110.3878) [src: Eq. 17];
- $w_{4}\equiv 2G_{4}-2XG_{5,\phi}-2XG_{5,X}\ddot{\phi}\,.$ (arXiv:1110.3878) [src: Eq. 20];
- $w_2$ and $w_3$, the latter carrying the X-derivatives of K, are the multi-row displays of Eqs. 18–19 of arXiv:1110.3878 (not transcribed here).

`[der]` The $w_1$ of Eq. 17 of arXiv:1110.3878 is, term by term, the $M_*^2$ of Eq. 4 of arXiv:1404.3713; Bellini & Sawicki's translation table lists $M_*^2$ as $w_1$ for the variables of Amendola et al. (their Ref. 10), which this dossier did not read.

The shift $\chi$ appears without time derivatives; its equation is the momentum constraint $w_{2}\Psi=2w_{1}\dot{\Phi}+\sum_{l=A,B}\rho_{l}(1+w_{l})v_{l}\,,$ (arXiv:1110.3878) [src: Eq. 21], which "can be used to integrate out the field Ψ". The action then takes the form $S^{(2)}=\int d^{4}x\left[{\cal A}_{ij}\dot{{\cal Q}}_{i}\dot{{\cal Q}}_{j}-{\cal C}_{ij}(\partial{\cal Q}_{i})(\partial{\cal Q}_{j})-{\cal B}_{ij}{\cal Q}_{i}\dot{{\cal Q}}_{j}-{\cal D}_{ij}{\cal Q}_{i}{\cal Q}_{j}\right],$ (arXiv:1110.3878) [src: Eq. 22] in the fields ${\cal Q}_i=(\Phi,v_A,v_B)$ [src: Sect. III, text after Eq. 22].

### Step 3 — ghost and gradient conditions in the $G_i$
<!-- key: step-3-ghost-and-gradient-conditions-in-the-gi -->

Requiring ${\cal A}$ to be positive definite gives the scalar no-ghost condition $Q_{S}\equiv\frac{w_{1}(4w_{1}w_{3}+9w_{2}^{2})}{3w_{2}^{2}}>0\,,$ (arXiv:1110.3878) [src: Eq. 23], together with $w_{A}(1+w_{A})\rho_{A}>0$ and the same for fluid B, "automatically satisfied for radiation ( $w_{A}=1/3$ ) and non-relativistic matter" [src: Sect. III, text after Eq. 23]. The speeds solve $\det\left(c_{s}^{2}{\cal A}-a^{2}{\cal C}\right)=0\,.$ (arXiv:1110.3878) [src: Eq. 24]; besides the two fluid speeds, the non-trivial root gives the no-Laplacian-instability condition $c_{S}^{2}\equiv\frac{3(2w_{1}^{2}w_{2}H-w_{2}^{2}w_{4}+4w_{1}w_{2}\dot{w}_{1}-2w_{1}^{2}\dot{w}_{2})-6w_{1}^{2}[(1+w_{A})\rho_{A}+(1+w_{B})\rho_{B}]}{w_{1}(4w_{1}w_{3}+9w_{2}^{2})}\geq 0\,.$ (arXiv:1110.3878) [src: Eq. 25]. For tensors, $Q_{T}\equiv\frac{w_{1}}{4}>0\,,$ [src: Eq. 27] and $c_{T}^{2}\equiv\frac{w_{4}}{w_{1}}\geq 0\,.$ (arXiv:1110.3878) [src: Eq. 28]; vector modes "do not add any conditions" [src: Sect. III, last paragraph]. The fluid densities enter $c_S^2$ explicitly; Bellini & Sawicki write that "in general Horndeski models superluminalities always exist somewhere in the phase space, especially in the presence of matter external to the scalar" (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Sect. 3.3].

### Step 4 — the α functions as combinations of the $G_i$
<!-- key: step-4-the-alpha-functions-as-combinations-of -->

Bellini & Sawicki: "The evolution of perturbations on a particular background is determined by four independent and dimensionless functions of time, $\alpha_{i}$, which can be determined for any particular Lagrangian by" their Eqs. 51–55 (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: App. A, text before Eq. 51]. $M_*^2$ and $\alpha_{\rm M}$ are Eqs. 51–52, in `formalism.md`.

The kineticity, braiding and tensor speed excess, three displays each copied whole with its rows joined in reading order, are $H^{2}M_{*}^{2}\alpha_{\textrm{K}}\equiv 2X\left(K_{X}+2XK_{XX}-2G_{3\phi}-2XG_{3\phi X}\right)++12\dot{\phi}XH\left(G_{3X}+XG_{3XX}-3G_{4\phi X}-2XG_{4\phi XX}\right)++12XH^{2}\left(G_{4X}+8XG_{4XX}+4X^{2}G_{4XXX}\right)--12XH^{2}\left(G_{5\phi}+5XG_{5\phi X}+2X^{2}G_{5\phi XX}\right)++4\dot{\phi}XH^{3}\left(3G_{5X}+7XG_{5XX}+2X^{2}G_{5XXX}\right)$, $HM_{*}^{2}\alpha_{\textrm{B}}\equiv 2\dot{\phi}\left(XG_{3X}-G_{4\phi}-2XG_{4\phi X}\right)++8XH\left(G_{4X}+2XG_{4XX}-G_{5\phi}-XG_{5\phi X}\right)++2\dot{\phi}XH^{2}\left(3G_{5X}+2XG_{5XX}\right)$ and $M_{*}^{2}\alpha_{\textrm{T}}\equiv 2X\left(2G_{4X}-2G_{5\phi}-\left(\ddot{\phi}-\dot{\phi}H\right)G_{5X}\right)$ (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Eqs. 53–55]. As printed, each row of Eqs. 53–54 ends with the sign that opens the next row, so the joined rows show "++" and "−−" at the row breaks; the sign is written twice, not applied twice: the Einstein–Boltzmann comparison reprints the same rows with one sign each (arXiv:1709.09135) [src: Eq. 8].

Which $G_i$ feed which function: $\alpha_{\rm K}$ "Contribution from all of K, $G_{3}$, $G_{4}$, $G_{5}$"; $\alpha_{\rm B}$ "Contributions from $G_{3}$, $G_{4}$, $G_{5}$"; $\alpha_{\rm M}$ and $\alpha_{\rm T}$ "Contributions from $G_{4}$ and $G_{5}$" (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Sect. 3.1]. "Given a fixed background and $\tilde{\rho}_{\text{m}0}$, any two trajectories in any two seemingly different models which have the same $\alpha_{i}$ exhibit absolutely no difference in their behaviour", strictly "only when the scalar rolls monotonically, i.e. $\dot{\phi}\neq 0$" [src: Sect. 3.1].

The same definitions in conformal time, with hi_class's normalisation and K written $G_2$, are $H^{2}M_{*}^{2}\alpha_{\textrm{K}}\equiv 2X\left(G_{2X}+2XG_{2XX}-2G_{3\phi}-2XG_{3\phi X}\right)+\frac{12H\phi^{\prime}X}{a}\left(G_{3X}+XG_{3XX}-3G_{4\phi X}-2XG_{4\phi XX}\right)+12H^{2}X\left[G_{4X}-G_{5\phi}+X\left(8G_{4XX}-5G_{5\phi X}\right)+2X^{2}\left(2G_{4XXX}-G_{5\phi XX}\right)\right]+\frac{4H^{3}\phi^{\prime}X}{a}\left(3G_{5X}+7XG_{5XX}+2X^{2}G_{5XXX}\right)$, $HM_{*}^{2}\alpha_{\textrm{B}}\equiv\frac{2\phi^{\prime}}{a}\left(XG_{3X}-G_{4\phi}-2XG_{4\phi X}\right)+8HX\left(G_{4X}+2XG_{4XX}-G_{5\phi}-XG_{5\phi X}\right)+\frac{2H^{2}\phi^{\prime}X}{a}\left(3G_{5X}+2XG_{5XX}\right)$ and $M_{*}^{2}\alpha_{\textrm{T}}\equiv 4X\left(G_{4X}-G_{5\phi}\right)-\frac{2}{a^{2}}\left(\phi^{\prime\prime}-2aH\phi^{\prime}\right)XG_{5X}\,.$ (arXiv:1605.06102) [src: Eqs. A.32–A.34].

`[der]` Up to writing the cosmic-time derivative of the field through the conformal one and grouping the terms differently, the hi_class braiding of Eq. A.33 of arXiv:1605.06102 has the same sign and normalisation as Eq. 54 of arXiv:1404.3713, so hi_class follows the Bellini–Sawicki convention; the hi_class paper itself states no relation.

### Step 5 — the stability conditions in the α basis
<!-- key: step-5-the-stability-conditions-in-the-alpha -->

Rewritten in the α functions, the scalar and tensor conditions become Eqs. 14–15 of arXiv:1404.3713, transcribed in `formalism.md#the-eft-of-dark-energy-and-parameterised`: positivity of D, of the scalar and tensor sound speeds squared and of the effective Planck mass. Bellini & Sawicki do not print the intermediate step from De Felice–Tsujikawa's $w_i$ to the α functions; their translation table lists the correspondence with the variables of De Felice, Kobayashi and Tsujikawa (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Table 2], see Step 6. Two statements fix how the conditions are used:

- "When $\alpha_{\textrm{B}}\neq 0$, the region of phase space where $D=0$ represents a pressure singularity [47] and no trajectory ever evolves across it [80]. However, nothing in the background dynamics prevents a trajectory with $c_{\text{s}}^{2}>0$ from crossing into an unstable region" (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Sect. 3.3].
- $c_{\rm s}$ is the speed "in the infinite frequency (eikonal) limit", "not a function of scale but only of time" [src: Sect. 3.3].

hi_class runs "four tests" on every background, in conformal time and its own normalisation: $Q_{\textrm{S}}=\frac{2M_{*}^{2}D}{\left(2-\alpha_{\textrm{B}}\right)^{2}}>0\,,\qquad\qquad D\equiv\alpha_{\textrm{K}}+\frac{3}{2}\alpha_{\textrm{B}}^{2}>0$ [src: Eq. 2.12], $c_{\text{s}}^{2}=\frac{1}{D}\left[\left(2-\alpha_{\textrm{B}}\right)\left(-\frac{H^{\prime}}{aH^{2}}+\frac{1}{2}\alpha_{\textrm{B}}\left(1+\alpha_{\textrm{T}}\right)+\alpha_{\textrm{M}}-\alpha_{\textrm{T}}\right)-\frac{3\left(\rho_{\textrm{m}}+p_{\textrm{m}}\right)}{H^{2}M_{*}^{2}}+\frac{\alpha_{\textrm{B}}^{\prime}}{aH}\right]>0$ [src: Eq. 2.13], $Q_{\textrm{T}}=\frac{M_{*}^{2}}{8}>0$ [src: Eq. 2.14] and $c_{\text{T}}^{2}=1+\alpha_{\textrm{T}}>0\,,$ (arXiv:1605.06102) [src: Eq. 2.15]. hi_class adds that D > 0 is needed "to avoid strong coupling problems or ghosts", D being "the demixed kinetic term of the additional scalar degree of freedom", and that tachyonic instabilities are not a hard test: "we choose to let the data exclude such cases" (arXiv:1605.06102) [src: Sects. 2.2–2.3].

## Variants and what they cost
<!-- key: variants-and-what-they-cost -->

- **ADM / unitary-gauge route (Gleyzes, Langlois and Vernizzi).** The quadratic action is written for a general Lagrangian of the lapse and of the geometric quantities of the uniform-field slices, which covers beyond-Horndeski and Hořava–Lifshitz theories (arXiv:1411.3712) [src: abstract]. The momentum constraint gives $\delta N=\frac{4{{\cal A}}_{K}}{{\cal B}+4H{{\cal A}}_{K}}\,\dot{\zeta}=\frac{\dot{\zeta}}{H\left(1+\alpha_{B}\right)}\,,$ (arXiv:1411.3712) [src: Eq. 77], and the no-ghost condition reads ${\cal L}_{\dot{\zeta}\dot{\zeta}}>0\quad\implies\quad\alpha=\alpha_{K}+6\alpha_{B}^{2}>0\;.$ (arXiv:1411.3712) [src: Eq. 83]. With matter, the sound speed is $c_{s}^{2}=-2\frac{(1+\alpha_{B})^{2}}{\alpha}\left\{1+\alpha_{T}-\frac{1+\alpha_{H}}{1+\alpha_{B}}\Big(1+\alpha_{M}-\frac{\dot{H}}{H^{2}}\Big)-\frac{1}{H}\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{1+\alpha_{H}}{1+\alpha_{B}}\right)\right\}-\frac{(1+\alpha_{H})^{2}}{\alpha}\,\frac{\rho_{\rm m}+p_{\rm m}}{M^{2}H^{2}}\;.$ (arXiv:1411.3712) [src: Eq. 85]. Cost and gain: one more function, $\alpha_{\rm H}$, "different from zero for theories that deviate from Horndeski theories" [src: Sect. 3.2.3, text after Eq. 82]; Bellini & Sawicki note that "In principle an extra property function would have to be added to our formulation to describe such theories" (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Sect. 3.5]. The braiding differs from Bellini & Sawicki's by the factor of footnote 8. <!-- num: not a value -->
- **EFT coefficients (Gubitosi et al.; EFTCAMB).** The EFT action mixes background and perturbations; Bellini & Sawicki claim that their basis "cleanly separates the properties of the background expansion ( $\dot{H}$ and $\Omega_{\text{m}0}$ ) from those of the perturbations ( $\alpha_{i}$ ), whereas the EFT approach mixes the two", and that the EFT approach "has some redundancy, which when removed reduces to the same number of degrees of freedom as we have in our minimal framework, five" (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Sect. 3.5]. In exchange the EFT basis reaches beyond Horndeski and Hořava gravity through $\gamma_4$, $\gamma_5$, $\gamma_6$ (arXiv:1709.09135) [src: Sect. II.1]. EFTCAMB's physical stability conditions cover only "the subset of the EFT corresponding to GLPV theories", the kinetic one being $\tilde{W}_{2}\left[4\tilde{W}_{1}\tilde{W}_{2}-\tilde{W}_{3}^{2}\right]>0,$ (arXiv:1405.3590) [src: Eq. 42], with the gradient condition of Eq. 43 and the $\tilde W_i$ of Eqs. 44–51; for $\gamma_3\neq-\gamma_4$ or $\gamma_6\neq0$ "the stability conditions need to be derived separately" [src: Sect. IV.6].
- **Inherently stable basis.** Replacing the α functions by quantities that are positive by construction: Lombriser et al. propose "physical basis of Planck mass evolution, sound speed of the scalar field fluctuation, kinetic coefficient, and background expansion, which covers Horndeski models with luminal speed of gravity", and find "no general limitations from stability requirements on the current values that can be assumed by the phenomenological modification of the Poisson equation and the gravitational slip besides the exclusion of anti-gravity" (arXiv:1810.05225, Lombriser et al. 2018) [src: abstract]. mochi_class implements it with the inputs $D_{\rm kin},\quad M_{\ast}^{2},\quad c_{\rm s}^{2},\quad\alpha_{{\rm B}0}\,,$ (arXiv:2407.11968, Cataneo & Bellini 2024) [src: Eq. 26] and recovers the braiding by integrating $\dot{\alpha}_{B}+\left(1-\alpha_{\rm M}+\frac{\dot{H}}{H}\right)\alpha_{\rm B}-\frac{\alpha_{\rm B}^{2}}{2}=\mathcal{S}(x)\,,$ [src: Eq. 27] with source $\mathcal{S}(x)\equiv-2\alpha_{\rm M}+c_{\rm sN}^{2}+\frac{\Delta M_{\ast}^{2}}{M_{\ast}^{2}}\frac{2\dot{H}}{H}-\frac{3(\rho_{\phi}+p_{\phi})}{M_{\ast}^{2}H^{2}}\,,$ (arXiv:2407.11968) [src: Eq. 28] and $c_{\rm sN}^{2}\equiv D_{\rm kin}c_{\rm s}^{2}$ (arXiv:2407.11968) [src: Sect. 2.1, inline, text after Eq. 28]. Cost: the braiding is an output of a non-linear ODE run backwards from $\alpha_{\rm B0}$, and the basis does not guarantee that the braiding avoids the $\alpha_{\rm B}=2$ discontinuity, which mochi_class tests separately [src: Sect. 3.1]. <!-- num: not a value --> mochi_class answers the objection that stable bases offer "no distinct advantages" (Denissenya & Linder 2018, as cited) with the case of designer f(R) [src: Sect. 2.1], see "What goes wrong". A 2025 application scans kinetic gravity braiding "while enforcing the absence of ghost and gradient instabilities from the outset" and finds "viable phantom-crossing solutions exist without conformal coupling" (arXiv:2512.13691, Cataneo & Koyama 2025) [src: abstract].
- **Code agreement.** For "a wide range of models and parameters", the general-purpose and model-specific solvers compared (EFTCAMB, hi_class, COOP and others) agree "at the sub-percent level" on the CMB angular spectrum and the dark-matter power spectrum (arXiv:1709.09135) [src: abstract]. <!-- num: not a value -->

## What goes wrong
<!-- key: what-goes-wrong -->

- **The factor −2 in the braiding.** Bellini–Sawicki and hi_class (Eq. 54; Eq. A.33) use one normalisation; EFTCAMB's RPH and Gleyzes et al. another, stated as $\alpha_{B}=-2\alpha_{B}^{\texttt{EFTCAMB}}$ (arXiv:1709.09135) [src: App. A, text after Eq. 42] and in footnote 8 of arXiv:1411.3712. It shows up as a different form of the same no-ghost condition, $D\equiv\alpha_{\textrm{K}}+\frac{3}{2}\alpha_{\textrm{B}}^{2}\,,$ (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Eq. 14] against $\alpha\equiv\alpha_{K}+6\alpha_{B}^{2}\;,$ (arXiv:1411.3712) [src: Eq. 80], and as braiding priors or posteriors of the opposite sign. Check: read the definition of $\alpha_{\rm B}$ in the paper or the code module that produced the number before comparing.
- **Sound speed computed from the α functions.** In designer f(R) on a ΛCDM background, which "is inherently stable ( $c_{\rm s}^{2}=1$ )", $c_s^2$ computed from the α functions, H and the background densities oscillates at early times: "cancellation errors further amplified by the small magnitude of $D_{\rm kin}$ in this model" "erroneously flag this viable model as unstable" (arXiv:2407.11968) [src: Fig. 1 caption]. <!-- eq: notation --> Check: a stable-basis code, or the covariant model, for models with small D.
- **The $\alpha_{\rm B}=2$ point.** $Q_{\rm S}$ carries $(2-\alpha_{\rm B})^{2}$ in its denominator (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Eq. 14]; mochi_class "always tests that the braiding never crosses 2, which was not implemented in hi_class", because of a discontinuity in the algebraic equation for $h'$ (arXiv:2407.11968) [src: Sect. 3.1 and footnote 5]. <!-- num: not a value -->
- **D = 0 crossed by a parameterisation.** D = 0 "is a pressure singularity and cannot be crossed in background dynamics, even though this might seem possible at the level of parameterised perturbations" (arXiv:1605.06102) [src: Sect. 2.2]. <!-- num: not a value -->
- **Oscillating scalar.** The α description needs $\dot\phi\neq0$; "If there are oscillations of the background values … the full model must be supplied and solved for" (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Sect. 3.1]. <!-- num: not a value -->
- **Stability checks beyond Horndeski in EFTCAMB.** The physical conditions of Eqs. 42–43 of arXiv:1405.3590 hold for GLPV; outside it "the user has to be careful in setting appropriate stability conditions for the chosen model" (arXiv:1405.3590) [src: Sect. IV.6].
- **Tachyonic instabilities.** hi_class does not reject them (arXiv:1605.06102) [src: Sect. 2.3]; mochi_class calls them "not necessarily harmful" and imposes only the absence of ghosts and gradient instabilities, $D_{\rm kin}>0,\quad M_{\ast}^{2}>0,\quad c_{\rm s}^{2}>0\,.$ (arXiv:2407.11968) [src: Eq. 22]. A fit that adds a tachyon prior is not comparable with one that does not.
- **Passing the linear tests is not health.** "It is also possible that theories which violate the null energy condition suffer from some sort of non-linear instability even when they do not exhibit the instabilities described above" (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Sect. 3.3]; hi_class repeats the caveat and lets the user "relax or disable these checks entirely" (arXiv:1605.06102) [src: Sect. 2.3].
- **Subluminality is a separate prior.** It is "a necessary precondition for there to exist a standard Wilsonian ultraviolet completion", yet "in general Horndeski models superluminalities always exist somewhere in the phase space" (arXiv:1404.3713) [src: Sect. 3.3]; positivity priors are in `results.md#theorems-and-no-go-results`. The effect of the gradient-stability prior on $w_0w_a$ posteriors is in `results.md#bounds-on-parameterised-frameworks` (arXiv:2606.31977).
