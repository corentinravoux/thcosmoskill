# Rubric for reviewing theoretical-cosmology prose

Scope: what to look for when reviewing a theory chapter, paper or proposal
section, and how to report it. Skeleton of the base release; the recurrent
slips, each with the paper that corrects it, are added in stage 6 of
`docs/plans/2026-10-30-plan-v2.md` from the domain skills.

Snapshot: 2026-10-30

## Red flags
<!-- key: red-flags -->

- An equation with no source and no derivation.
- Conventions mixed between sections, or an equation copied from a source
  with other conventions without saying so.
- A model called "ruled out", "favoured", "natural" or "fine-tuned" without
  the dataset, the confidence level or the criterion.
- An approximation used outside the range in which its source validates it
  (slow roll, quasi-static, sub-horizon, Limber, tree level).
- A theoretical consistency condition (ghost, gradient instability, strong
  coupling, positivity) not checked for a model presented as viable.
- A forecast presented next to a measurement without a label.

## Calibrating a finding
<!-- key: calibrating-a-finding -->

Report each finding with the line it is on, what is wrong, the source that
shows it, and how much it matters for the argument of the section. A
convention stated once and applied consistently is not a finding, even if
another convention is more common.
