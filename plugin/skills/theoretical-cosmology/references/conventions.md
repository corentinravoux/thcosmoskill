# Conventions to pin down in a theory text

Scope: the conventions a theoretical-cosmology text must state, and the
places where the literature is split. Skeleton of the base release: the
table of which reference work adopts which convention, each with its
source, is written at step P4 of `docs/plans/2026-10-30-plan-v2.md`. Until
then this file lists what to check, not who uses what.

Snapshot: 2026-10-30

## Spacetime and gravity
<!-- key: spacetime-and-gravity -->

- Metric signature, and the signs of the Riemann tensor, the Ricci tensor
  and the Einstein equations; state all three.
- Units: natural units or not; reduced or full Planck mass; where 8πG
  appears.
- Time variable: cosmic time, conformal time, e-folds, and the direction in
  which e-folds are counted.
- Curvature parameter and the sign of Ω_k.

## Perturbations
<!-- key: perturbations -->

- Gauge, and the names given to the metric potentials (several
  incompatible naming schemes are in use).
- Which curvature perturbation (ζ or R), and its sign.
- Fourier convention, the factor of (2π)³, and whether the power spectrum
  is dimensional, P(k), or dimensionless, Δ²(k).
- Initial conditions: adiabatic or isocurvature, and their normalisation.

## Early universe
<!-- key: early-universe -->

- Slow-roll parameters: Hubble-flow or potential definitions, and the order
  at which predictions are given.
- Sign and normalisation convention of f_NL, and which shape template.
- Tensor-to-scalar ratio: the pivot scale.

## Structure formation and dark sector
<!-- key: structure-formation-and-dark-sector -->

- Bias basis and the normalisation of each operator.
- Perturbation-theory kernels: the time dependence assumed.
- Dark-energy and modified-gravity parameterisations: which α_i, μ–Σ or EFT
  basis, and its sign conventions.
- Units of lengths: Mpc or Mpc/h; of wavenumbers: h/Mpc or 1/Mpc.
