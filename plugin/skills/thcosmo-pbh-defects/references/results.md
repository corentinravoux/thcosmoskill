# Results on primordial black holes and topological defects

Scope: results that hold independently of one model — collapse thresholds and mass scaling from simulations, statistical results on abundance and clustering, evaporation results, network-scaling results and bounds on defect parameters — with the assumptions under which each was obtained, and the history of the key results. Predictions of one model are in `models.md`. Measured PBH limits as such live in cosmoskill (`cosmo-early-universe-exotica`, `cosmo-neutrinos-dark-matter`).

## History of key results
<!-- key: history-of-key-results -->

- Threshold: Carr's Jeans-length estimate $\delta_{\mathrm{c},\mspace{1.5mu}\rm Carr}=w$ (quoted in arXiv:2211.05767) [src: Eq. II.31] → Harada, Yoo & Kohri 2013, "a much better agreement with the result of recent numerical simulations" (arXiv:1309.4201) [src: abstract, inline] → shape dependence through the compaction function (arXiv:1809.02127) [src: abstract, inline] → averaged-compaction universality for radiation (arXiv:1907.13311) [src: abstract, inline], extended to $w\gtrsim 1/3$ (arXiv:2007.05564) [src: abstract, inline] → 3+1 tests: consistency for a spherical Gaussian (arXiv:2608.13206) and breakdown of the one-peak-one-PBH map for neighbouring peaks (arXiv:2609.03051) [src: abstract, inline]. None of the later results supersedes HYK for its own variable; the threshold-estimation scheme of the review read is built on "the remarkable result of References [58, 78] that the averaged critical compaction function is a universal quantity independent on the curvature profile considered (upon deviation at per-cent-level)" (arXiv:2211.05767) [src: Sect. II.7, inline, first paragraph]. <!-- num: not a value -->
- Mass: one horizon mass → critical scaling observed in radiation-fluid collapse (arXiv:gr-qc/9402041) and applied to PBHs (arXiv:astro-ph/9709072) [src: abstract] → discrete self-similarity imprints for scalar-field collapse (arXiv:2608.21834) [src: abstract].
- Statistics: thresholding $\mathcal R_c$ superseded by the density contrast (arXiv:1405.7023) [src: abstract] and by peak theory with a local density threshold (arXiv:1805.03946) [src: abstract] → nonlinear $\delta$–$\zeta$ relation (arXiv:1904.00970; arXiv:1904.00984) [src: abstract] → non-perturbative local non-Gaussianity (arXiv:2211.01728) [src: abstract] → excursion-set and broad-spectrum systematics (arXiv:2509.25871; arXiv:2603.23025) [src: abstract].
- Evaporation: semiclassical lifetimes and $M_*$ (arXiv:2002.12778) [src: Sect. I.4.1] → memory-burden hypothesis (arXiv:2006.00011) [src: abstract] and the re-opened light-PBH window under it (arXiv:2402.14069; arXiv:2402.17823) [src: abstract]. The semiclassical bounds are not superseded unless the memory-burden hypothesis is adopted. <!-- num: not a value -->
- Strings: friction era and scaling, small loops in early Nambu–Goto simulations, then large-loop families "typically with $l/t\sim 0.1$" (as reported in arXiv:0911.1345) [src: Sect. 4, inline, fifth paragraph] → field-theory loops that decay fast (arXiv:1703.06696; arXiv:2103.16248) [src: abstract, inline] → PTA exclusion of stable GUT-scale strings and the rise of metastable strings (arXiv:2307.04691) [src: Sect. 1].
- Domain walls: $\mathcal{A}\simeq 0.8\pm 0.1,$ from field-theory simulations (arXiv:1703.02576) [src: Eq. 2.18] → initial-condition dependence and "$\xi_{max} \approx 1.2$" (arXiv:2509.25367) [src: abstract]. The 2025 value is proposed under the assumption that the memory of initial conditions is erased; it does not yet replace the earlier one by consensus (`[der]`).
