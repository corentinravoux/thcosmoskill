## Single-field models

### Canonical single-field slow-roll inflation
- **Defining equations:** $S_{2}=\int dtd^{3}x~\left[a^{3}\epsilon\dot{\zeta}^{2}-a\epsilon(\partial\zeta)^{2}\right]~$ for the comoving curvature perturbation, in units of the reduced Planck mass (Chen 2010, arXiv:1002.1416) [src: Eq. 4.8]; the covariant action of that paper is printed with a slip (formalism.md, "Not verified").
- **Parameters:** the Hubble rate $H$ and the Hubble-flow parameters $\epsilon$ and $\eta$ of Chen 2010, arXiv:1002.1416 [src: Eq. 4.2], all evaluated at horizon exit [src: Sect. 4, text after Eq. 4.18].
- **Predictions:** $P_{\zeta}=\frac{H^{2}}{8\pi^{2}\epsilon}~$ (arXiv:1002.1416) [src: Eq. 4.18]; bispectrum of order the slow-roll parameters, $f_{NL}\sim{\cal O}(0.01)$ for these models (arXiv:1002.1416) [src: Sect. 4, text after Eq. 4.23]; full shape in formalism.md (arXiv:1002.1416) [src: Eqs. 4.22–4.23]; squeezed limit fixed by the tilt (arXiv:astro-ph/0407059) [src: Eq. 1].
- **Consistency:** the squeezed-limit bispectrum predicted by the model produces no primordial signal in the squeezed CMB bispectrum or in scale-dependent halo bias, only projection effects (arXiv:1305.0824, abstract); reply: none found.
